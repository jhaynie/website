I’d like to give you an update about what I’ve been working for the past few months [since departing Appcelerator](https://medium.com/@jhaynie/appcelerator-so-long-and-thanks-for-all-the-fish-f5ac819e3098#.r3rw20nib).

Nolan (who co-founded [Appcelerator](http://www.appcelerator.com) with me) and I joined together with our friend, [John Saddington](https://medium.com/@saddington), to start a new venture. Right now, we’re calling it [Pinpoint](https://pinpt.co) (or pinpt for short since you can’t seem to get any reasonable domain name anymore).

And we’ve been super busy getting ready for today … we launched our private alpha release today!! And I’m so excited. *It feels so great starting something brand new and shipping product.*

It also is a chance to execute against these [five things](https://medium.com/welcome-to-your-startup-life/do-good-and-do-business-well-5dd4f3c08dee#.w0v9qk1yp) that I’ve wanted to do in my next venture as well as build my [first-ever B Corporation](https://medium.com/welcome-to-your-startup-life/do-good-and-do-business-well-5dd4f3c08dee#.hb2myhy6z). I hope you follow along here!

So what are we doing?

> We’re focusing on building a product for high performance engineering teams to provide actionable analytics and to help them build better software.

### We’re calling this #EngOps.

If you’re a company which employs people to build software — either for yourself or others — either insourced or outsourced — we think we can help you and your team achieve better ROI and have more fun doing it. Join us on this journey by signing up to [join the private beta for early access](https://saddington.typeform.com/to/kzE7LA).

You can also follow our [blog](https://blog.pinpt.co) or [twitter](https://twitter.com/WhatIsPinpoint) account for more information. Stay tuned for a lot of fun stuff coming. You can also follow me on twitter at [@jhaynie](https://twitter.com/jhaynie).
